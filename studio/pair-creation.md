# Pair creation

Pair programming is two people at one keyboard: one drives, one navigates, both think. The Right One is made the same way, by a human and an AI agent.

| | Peyton | Ember (AI) |
|---|---|---|
| Brings | the story and the writing, taste and direction, the final yes | its own angle on each idea, structure, timing, the code |
| Works by | saying yes or no to every step, in her own words | one idea of its own and one cut per answer; a rebel version for every concept; disagrees once, then builds her choice |
| Runs in | | Claude Code (Anthropic), an agentic coding tool: writes and runs the code, renders frames, reads them back |

Around the pair, a small studio of agents: one holds the practice (the rules, the journals, the tools); one reviews each work blind, then with the intent, and scores it; small tools read the music for tempo, bars, energy and cues.

## The twelve steps

```
 1 SEED        a piece of writing, a feeling, a memory
 2 ANSWER      Ember's own angle: what it sees, one thing to add, one to cut
 3 CONCEPT     the title (from the writing), the idea in one line, the pair
 4 REFERENCES  looked at for principle, never copied
 5 MUSIC       picked before any motion, read for beats and cues
 6 SCORE       every bar with its word, motion, light and mark
 7 SPEC        approved before any code
 8 STILLS      the key moments, picked before any video
 9 VIDEO       built in code, timed to the track
10 REVIEW      a blind outside eye, then informed, scored
11 YES         signed, the cover, the files
12 RECORD      the journal and the rules, updated
```

Nothing moves to the next step without Peyton's yes.
